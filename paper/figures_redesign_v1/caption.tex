\caption{Frozen-policy performance on common500 with $N=1024$ complex channel
uses at the measured SNRs of 4, 10, and 19 dB. Markers show means over 500 source
images, after averaging the three registered noise realizations within each
source; all failure outcomes remain in the denominator. Error bars are the
published pointwise 95\% source-bootstrap confidence intervals (10,000 resamples,
seed 2026100701), reused without new resampling. Straight segments connect only
measured points. The entropy-coded NeST-Com curve uses the partial-scale family:
its 19 dB point uses the corrected frozen m10 T1-C result, while 4/10 dB and the
other methods retain their original frozen results. LPIPS uses AlexNet;
DreamSim uses the registered ensemble. Prediction agreement is ConvNeXt top-1
agreement with the source-image prediction, not classification accuracy. The
latent-space JSCC and adapted SwinJSCC checkpoints are 100k and 200k, respectively.
Some confidence intervals are shorter than the markers or overlap.}
