# Fig. 3 qualitative comparison

In Fig. 3(a), NeST-Com and Latent JSCC preserve the arrangement and curved outlines of the pastries, while folds are more distinct in the NeST-Com reconstruction. In Fig. 3(b), both retain the dog's seated silhouette; NeST-Com shows more distinct facial and fur detail. SwinJSCC produces smoother structures, BPG + LDPC exhibits block artifacts, and ARPC + LDPC produces fine textures with visible changes in object shape or appearance. These selected development examples illustrate reconstruction differences rather than population-level performance.

Fig.3(a) 中，NeST-Com 和 Latent JSCC 保留了糕点的排列与弧形轮廓，NeST-Com 的糕点褶皱更清楚。Fig.3(b) 中，两者都保留了狗坐立的轮廓，NeST-Com 的面部与毛发细节更清晰。SwinJSCC 的细节较平滑，BPG + LDPC 出现块状伪影；ARPC + LDPC 呈现较细的纹理，但物体形状或外观存在可见变化。这两个选定的 development 案例用于展示重建差异，不代表总体性能结论。
