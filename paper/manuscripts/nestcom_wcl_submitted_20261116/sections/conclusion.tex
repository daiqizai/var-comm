\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com pairs actual-length within-scale arithmetic prefix termination---guaranteed feasible as an initial segment by a coder-specific nondecreasing-length property---with receiver-side known-token overwrite in VAR. On 500 ImageNet images at $N=1024$ and 10 dB, NeST-Com lifts source-bit capacity utilization from 77.70\% to 99.79\% and lowers DISTS and DreamSim by 8.7\% and 20.5\% over a shared-model whole-scale baseline, while the Eighth grid retains most of the gain at reduced latency. Combining multi-codeblock unequal error protection with quantized cross-device priors is a natural direction for fading wireless links.
