\begin{abstract}
NeST-Com is a digital wireless image communication framework transmitting next-scale residual token descriptions under fixed per-image symbol budgets. Actual-length frame fitting terminates arithmetic codes at every admissible within-scale prefix, selecting the longest prefix fitting the protected frame body. Received boundary tokens enter the visual autoregressive (VAR) model's known-token overwrite and recursive state update, correcting residuals and conditioning finer scales. On 500 ImageNet images at 1024 symbols and 10 dB, NeST-Com raises source-bit capacity utilization from 77.70\% to 99.79\% and reduces DISTS and DreamSim by 8.7\% and 20.5\% versus a shared-model whole-scale baseline; matched controls quantify quality--computation tradeoffs.
\end{abstract}
