\begin{abstract}
Next-scale residual tokens provide a coarse-to-fine image description, but whole-scale endpoints can underfill fixed digital frames. NeST-Com fits independently terminated, entropy-coded within-scale prefixes and uses received tokens for residual reconstruction and finer-scale conditioning. With shared model components, PSNR improves over whole-scale transmission by 0.32--0.70~dB at 1024 symbols and 0.39--0.79~dB at 512 symbols. Source-bit utilization rises from 77.70\% to 95.21\% at 1024 symbols and 10~dB. At 512 symbols, NeST-Com exceeds a fixed-frame Ada-TokenCom adaptation by 1.74--4.18~dB PSNR; Ada achieves higher CLIP-I throughout and lower DreamSim at 19~dB.
\end{abstract}
