\begin{abstract}
NeST-Com is a digital image communication framework that uses next-scale residual descriptions under fixed per-image symbol budgets. It coordinates source-prefix selection, channel protection and prefix-constrained reconstruction through actual-length frame fitting. Independently terminated within-scale prefixes retain scale-wise probability evaluation, while received boundary tokens refine the residual state and finer-scale predictions. At 1024 symbols and 10 dB, source-bit utilization increases from 77.70\% to 99.79\%, reducing DISTS and DreamSim by 8.7\% and 20.5\% relative to shared-model whole-scale transmission. Matched controls and complete-system comparisons characterize reconstruction gains and quality--cost tradeoffs.
\end{abstract}
