\begin{abstract}
NeST-Com transmits images as next-scale residual tokens under a fixed per-image symbol budget. Actual-length frame fitting independently terminates arithmetic codes at each admissible within-scale prefix and selects the longest prefix that fits the protected frame body. The visual autoregressive (VAR) model uses known-token overwrite and recursive state updates to correct residuals and condition finer-scale predictions on received boundary tokens. On 500 ImageNet images at 1024 symbols and 10 dB, NeST-Com raises source-bit capacity utilization from 77.70\% to 99.79\% and reduces DISTS and DreamSim by 8.7\% and 20.5\% relative to a shared-model whole-scale baseline. Matched controls quantify the quality--computation tradeoff.
\end{abstract}
