\subsection{Operation Near the Channel Threshold}
With H10 policies fixed, 100 sources and 20 noise realizations yield 30\,000 receptions over 8--10 dB. All methods reject every frame at 8 dB. WHOLE/NeST/Ada rejection rates are 15.75/14.95/13.50\% at 9 dB, 0.10--0.20\% at 9.5 dB and zero observed at 10 dB. Quality metrics include gray outputs from rejected frames (S5).
