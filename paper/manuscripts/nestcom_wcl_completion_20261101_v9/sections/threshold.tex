\subsection{Operation Near the Channel Threshold}
With fixed 10-dB timing policies and actual noise variance at RX, 100 images and 20 noise realizations yield 30\,000 receptions over 8--10 dB. All methods reject every frame at 8 dB. WHOLE/NeST/Ada rejection rates are 15.75/14.95/13.50\% at 9 dB, 0.10--0.20\% at 9.5 dB and zero at 10 dB. Metrics retain all rejected gray outputs.
