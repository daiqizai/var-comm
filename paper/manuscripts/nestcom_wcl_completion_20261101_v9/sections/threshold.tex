Near the channel threshold, the three token systems use fixed 10-dB timing policies, and RX uses the actual noise variance. Testing 100 images with 20 noise realizations per image at each of five SNRs from 8 to 10 dB gives 30\,000 receptions. All three methods reject every frame at 8 dB. At 9 dB, rejection rates are 15.75\%, 14.95\% and 13.50\% for WHOLE, NeST and Ada, respectively. They fall to 0.10--0.20\% at 9.5 dB and to zero at 10 dB. All rejected gray outputs remain in the metric denominators.
