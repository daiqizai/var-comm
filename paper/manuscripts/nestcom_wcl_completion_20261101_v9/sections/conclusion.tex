\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com retains scale-wise prediction while fitting within-scale transmission endpoints to fixed digital frames. Independently terminated streams permit actual-length selection, and the received prefix enters the recursive residual state used for reconstruction and finer-scale prediction. Matched controls show that finer endpoints convert unused source capacity into improved reconstruction, with a measured cost for additional granularity. The complete system offers favorable source-referenced reconstruction at tight budgets, while perceptual rankings vary with budget and receiver choice.
