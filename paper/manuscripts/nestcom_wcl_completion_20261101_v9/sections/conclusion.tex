\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com fits within-scale prefixes by arithmetic-code length to carry more source information in fixed protected frames. Shared-model comparisons and matched controls link fuller capacity use to better reconstruction. Eighth and Token offer quality--computation choices, with finer termination yielding additional measured gains at higher TX cost. System-level comparisons show Ada's perceptual advantages at some operating points and SwinJSCC's substantially faster processing.
