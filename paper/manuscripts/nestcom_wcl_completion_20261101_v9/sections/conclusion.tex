\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com uses incomplete next-scale boundaries in fixed digital frames through independently terminated prefixes, actual-length fitting and prefix-constrained reconstruction. Shared-model and matched controls show that delivered boundary tokens improve residual reconstruction and finer-scale conditioning. Token-wise fitting maximizes retained source-token count, while coarser within-scale grids offer lower processing cost. The evaluated system provides favorable source-referenced reconstruction at tight budgets, with budget-dependent perceptual rankings and explicit processing-cost choices.
