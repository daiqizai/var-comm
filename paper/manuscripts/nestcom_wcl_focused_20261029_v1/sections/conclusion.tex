\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com adapts next-scale residual representations to fixed digital frames through within-scale endpoints, actual-length fitting, and prefix-constrained reconstruction. Received boundary tokens improve both residual reconstruction and finer-scale prediction. Shared-model experiments show higher source-bit utilization and consistent PSNR gains at 512- and 1024-symbol budgets, while comparisons with existing systems demonstrate useful low-budget reconstruction performance. Future work will extend frame selection to uncertain channel conditions.
