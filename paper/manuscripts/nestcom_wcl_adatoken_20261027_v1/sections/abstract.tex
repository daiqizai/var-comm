\begin{abstract}
Complete-scale endpoints can leave unused capacity in fixed-budget image communication. NeST-Com combines within-scale token endpoints, fitting by actual terminated entropy-code length, and prefix-constrained reconstruction with a shared frozen next-scale prior. On 500 ImageNet images, within-scale transmission improves PSNR by 0.32--0.70~dB over complete-scale transmission with the same model components; source-bit utilization at 10~dB rises from 77.70\% to 95.21\%. Controlled endpoint diagnostics and Kodak evaluation support this mechanism. A six-system comparison shows higher PSNR than a fixed-frame Ada-TokenCom adaptation, while Ada achieves better perceptual or feature-similarity scores at several operating points.
\end{abstract}
