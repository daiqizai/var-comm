这说明外部优势与“可传源信息较少”的工作区间有关。
它不是更强的无线抗噪证据；相同信息预算去掉PHY后仍存在相同方向的低预算优势。
它提供采用next-scale系统的实用理由，完整模型差异仍存在，因此不写成顺序单变量因果证明。

最直观的机制例子：
B从1212增加到1302 bits，增加90bit。
WHOLE平均源比特915.858 -> 918.954，只增加3.096bit；
NeST 1150.046 -> 1239.950，增加89.904bit。
WHOLE DreamSim 0.204857 -> 0.204600，几乎停滞；
NeST 0.183663 -> 0.174254，继续改善。

这直接说明整尺度端点形成容量平台，而细端点把额外预算转为实际源描述。
“89.9 of the additional 90 bits on average”可以在主文使用；不能解释成每图精确保证。

5.1 Abstract（按空白分词99词）
----------------------------------------
Next-scale residual tokens support source-referenced perceptual reconstruction from coarse descriptions, but whole-scale transmission can underfill fixed digital frames. NeST-Com fits independently terminated token-wise prefixes to the available capacity and uses received boundary tokens for residual correction and finer-scale conditioning. At 1024 symbols and 10 dB, source-bit utilization rises from 77.70% to 99.79%, reducing DISTS and DreamSim by 8.7% and 20.5% relative to shared-model whole-scale transmission. Equal-information-budget tests favor NeST-Com over adapted Ada-TokenCom on both distances at the three smallest tested budgets. At the same symbol/SNR setting, transmitter-plus-receiver processing takes 0.66 s versus 15.42 s for Ada on matched hardware.

5.2 Introduction（四段草案）
----------------------------------------
A hard per-image channel-symbol budget must accommodate source information, signaling, and protection. This work focuses on preserving source-image structure and perceptual similarity under this constraint. We primarily evaluate reconstruction using DISTS and DreamSim, while retaining PSNR to quantify pixelwise fidelity. In generative image communication, the transmitted description constrains reconstruction to the source, while a learned prior predicts untransmitted content. Both the organization of this description and its use of the available payload capacity therefore affect reconstruction quality.

Next-scale tokenization organizes an image into complete coarse maps followed by residual refinements \cite{var}. The coarse maps describe the full latent grid, and finer maps progressively refine it. Token probabilities within a scale share the completed coarse-scale context, enabling scale-wise probability evaluation and completion. ARPC applies next-scale entropy coding and generation to progressive image compression \cite{arpc}, while Ada-TokenCom combines next-token prefix coding, tail generation, and wireless resource adaptation \cite{adatokencom}. These developments motivate examining next-scale representations under a hard per-image digital-frame budget.

Progressive source transmission has long considered the matching of source descriptions to channel resources \cite{chande}. For next-scale tokens, a complete residual map can exceed the remaining frame capacity even when a substantial part of that map would fit. Image-dependent entropy lengths further prevent a fixed token count from specifying the actual bit requirement. Exploiting the remaining capacity therefore requires independently decodable within-scale endpoints and actual terminated-length fitting. The delivered boundary tokens must also update the residual representation and the conditioning of finer-scale predictions.

NeST-Com combines token-wise endpoints, independently terminated arithmetic coding, and prefix-constrained reconstruction in a fixed digital frame. Shared-model comparisons and matched controls show improvements in both pixelwise fidelity and source-referenced perceptual similarity from retaining and using boundary-scale information. At 1024 symbols and 10 dB, source-bit utilization increases from 77.70\% to 99.79\%. Equal-information-budget comparisons identify a favorable low-budget regime for source-referenced perceptual reconstruction, and measurements on matched hardware show a substantially shorter processing time than the fixed-frame Ada-TokenCom implementation. Together, these results establish the practical value of the next-scale system and the contribution of its frame-fitting design.

5.3 Method：端点选择和实际执行一致的替换句
----------------------------------------
With the MCS fixed, the transmitter evaluates the independently terminated lengths of the allowed endpoints up to the calibrated target and selects the feasible endpoint with the largest retained-token count. Scale-wise probabilities and the incremental arithmetic-coding traversal are reused across candidate terminations. The length test uses the actual terminated stream, without assuming monotonicity of code length in token count.

e*(x,a) = argmax_{e in E, T(e)<=T(e_target), b(x,e)+29<=k_a} T(e).

5.4 III.B 机制结果草案
----------------------------------------
Table~\ref{tab:internal}(A) compares NeST-Com with whole-scale transmission using shared model components. Across all seven budget/SNR settings, NeST-Com improves both pixelwise fidelity and source-referenced perceptual similarity: all five reconstruction metrics favor it in the source-paired intervals. At \(N=1024\) and 10 dB, source-bit utilization increases from 77.70\% to 99.79\%, while DISTS decreases from 0.1621 to 0.1481 and DreamSim from 0.1317 to 0.1048. The PSNR gain ranges from 0.45 to 0.88 dB across the four \(N=1024\) points.

Table~\ref{tab:internal}(B) isolates endpoint granularity under the same model, 16-QAM rate-2/3 link, maximum position \((8,126)\), and channel realizations. Refining Whole, Quarter, Eighth, and Token endpoints raises utilization from 78.13\% through 95.19\% and 97.44\% to 99.85\%. Relative to Quarter, Token improves PSNR by \(0.172\,[0.125,0.221]\) dB and reduces DISTS by \(0.0030\,[0.0020,0.0041]\) and DreamSim by \(0.0028\,[0.0006,0.0050]\). Thus, finer legal endpoints convert otherwise unused capacity into improved reconstruction.

Table~\ref{tab:internal}(C) uses the same actually received prefixes to examine their successive reconstruction effects. A0 retains prior completion, B0 adds the received boundary residual, and C0 also updates finer-scale conditioning. At 10 dB, residual correction increases PSNR by 0.287 dB and reduces DreamSim by 0.0113. Updating the subsequent context provides a further 0.537 dB PSNR gain and a 0.0159 DreamSim reduction. Both stages have favorable source-paired intervals. These results show that boundary information contributes through both the current residual and subsequent prediction.

5.5 III.C 外部质量、预算及成本草案
----------------------------------------
Across the four tested SNRs at \(N=1024\), NeST-Com achieves lower DISTS and DreamSim than ARPC, SwinJSCC, Latent JSCC, and BPG, with all corresponding source-paired intervals excluding zero. SwinJSCC and BPG achieve higher PSNR at all four points. This comparison shows a tradeoff between pixelwise fidelity and source-referenced perceptual similarity under the same symbol budget.

The closest perceptual competitor is the fixed-frame Ada-TokenCom adaptation. NeST-Com improves both distances at 4 dB and DISTS at 7 dB; the 7-dB DreamSim interval overlaps zero. Ada yields lower values on both distances at 10 dB and lower DISTS at 19 dB, where the DreamSim interval again overlaps zero. NeST-Com has higher PSNR than Ada at all four points. These are complete-system comparisons under the common symbol budget.

An error-free diagnostic separates source-information budget effects from wireless decoding and MCS choices. At six total information budgets from 620 to 2577 bits, NeST-Com improves all five metrics over WHOLE with favorable source-paired intervals. Against adapted Ada, DISTS favors NeST-Com at 620, 1212, 1302, and 1940 bits, and DreamSim favors it at the first three points. Ada is better on both distances at 2248 and 2577 bits. This identifies a favorable low-budget regime for the next-scale system; the full curves are provided in Supplement~S4.

The same diagnostic also exposes the capacity plateaus caused by whole-scale endpoints. Increasing the budget from 1212 to 1302 bits adds only 3.1 source bits on average to WHOLE. Token-wise fitting uses 89.9 of the additional 90 bits, with DreamSim decreasing from 0.1837 to 0.1743, while WHOLE remains near 0.2047. This connects finer endpoint granularity directly to the effective use of additional source capacity.

The selected examples in Fig.~\ref{fig:qualitative} illustrate source-structure preservation at 7 dB. NeST-Com retains the television-cabinet layout and antenna arrangement, and recovers a more rectangular buckle in the patterned-leather example. WHOLE rounds the buckle, while Ada introduces cabinet details or lettering absent from the source. The full same-source comparisons are available in Supplement~S6.

Table~\ref{tab:external} reports complete transmitter and receiver processing on matched hardware. At \(N=1024\) and 10 dB, NeST-Com takes 248.4 ms at TX and 411.6 ms at RX, giving 0.660 s in total, compared with 15.422 s for the fixed-frame Ada implementation. The processing-time ratio is 23.4. Relative to WHOLE, NeST-Com increases total processing from 583.7 to 660.0 ms. Under the matched endpoint ceiling, Quarter-to-Token refinement raises TX time from 214.1 to 248.4 ms alongside the improvement in source-capacity utilization from 95.19\% to 99.85\%. SwinJSCC and Latent JSCC require substantially less processing, illustrating the quality--computation tradeoff among the evaluated systems.

5.6 III.D N512草案
----------------------------------------
At \(N=512\), NeST-Com improves all five metrics over WHOLE at each tested SNR, with every source-paired interval excluding zero. At 10 dB, both systems use target \((7,0)\) and 16-QAM rate 2/3; token-wise fitting raises source-bit utilization from 79.30\% to 99.54\%. Compared with adapted Ada, NeST-Com achieves higher PSNR and lower LPIPS throughout, and lower DISTS and DreamSim at 4 and 10 dB. Ada gives lower values on the latter two distances at 19 dB. The reduced-symbol-budget results therefore agree with the low-information-budget operating region identified above.

5.7 III.E 门限草案
----------------------------------------
With the 10-dB policies fixed, 100 sources and 20 noise realizations per point yield 30,000 receptions across actual SNRs of 8--10 dB. All three methods reject every frame at 8 dB. At 9 dB, rejection rates are 15.75\%, 14.95\%, and 13.50\% for WHOLE, NeST-Com, and Ada, respectively; these fall to 0.10--0.20\% at 9.5 dB, with no observed rejection at 10 dB. Overall quality includes rejected-frame gray outputs, and successful-only quality is reported in Supplement~S5. The gains from source reconstruction are thus realized within the reliable operating range of the selected digital-link policy.

5.9 Conclusion草案
----------------------------------------
NeST-Com uses next-scale residual descriptions for source-referenced perceptual reconstruction under fixed digital-frame budgets. Token-wise endpoints, actual terminated-length fitting, and prefix-constrained reconstruction retain and use additional source information. Shared-model and matched controls show improvements in both pixelwise fidelity and perceptual similarity, with boundary tokens contributing through residual correction and finer-scale conditioning. Equal-information-budget comparisons identify favorable perceptual reconstruction at tight budgets, while matched-hardware measurements show substantially shorter processing time than the tested Ada adaptation. These findings support next-scale communication as a practical design for resource-limited perceptual image reconstruction.
