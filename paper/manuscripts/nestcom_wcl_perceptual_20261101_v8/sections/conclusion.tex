\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com retains and uses additional source information through token-wise endpoints, actual-length fitting and prefix-constrained reconstruction. Shared-model and matched controls improve both pixel fidelity and perceptual similarity, with received boundary tokens correcting residuals and finer-scale conditioning. Equal-information-budget tests identify a favorable tight-budget regime, and matched-hardware tests show shorter processing than the Ada adaptation. These findings support next-scale perceptual reconstruction within the reliable range of the selected digital-link policy.
