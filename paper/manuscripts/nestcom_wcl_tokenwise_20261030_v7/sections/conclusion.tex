\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com fits token-wise next-scale prefixes to fixed digital frames using actual independently terminated code lengths, then uses received tokens for residual correction and finer-scale conditioning. Shared-model comparisons and matched controls support improved source-bit utilization and source-referenced reconstruction. Complete-system results show a low-budget advantage over the fixed-frame Ada adaptation alongside metric-dependent tradeoffs at larger budgets. Reliability remains tied to the chosen digital-link policy.
