\begin{abstract}
Next-scale residual tokens provide a coarse-to-fine image description, but whole-scale endpoints can underfill fixed digital frames. NeST-Com fits independently terminated token-wise prefixes to the actual frame capacity and uses received boundary tokens for residual reconstruction and finer-scale conditioning. With shared model components, it improves PSNR over whole-scale transmission by 0.45--0.88 dB at 1024 symbols and 0.50--0.99 dB at 512 symbols, with lower DISTS and DreamSim at all seven tested points. At 1024 symbols and 10 dB, source-bit utilization increases from 77.70\% to 99.79\%. External comparisons reveal budget-dependent perceptual tradeoffs.
\end{abstract}
