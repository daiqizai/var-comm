\section{Proposed NeST-Com Framework}
\subsection{Next-Scale Representation and Digital Frame}
We transmit $x\in[0,1]^{256\times256\times3}$ over complex AWGN,
\begin{equation}
 y=s+n,\quad n\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,\sigma^2 I_N),\quad
 \mathrm{SNR}=10\log_{10}(E_s/\sigma^2),
 \label{eq:channel}
\end{equation}
with $N=1024$ complex symbols per image and nominal $E_s=2$. The channel-use ratio to RGB samples is $1/192$. In the matched-SNR experiments, TX selects its frozen policy using the channel SNR; RX uses the true noise variance for LLRs. Digital constellations have average energy two without per-frame renormalization. Continuous JSCC data are normalized per image to average energy two; required Swin side information is separately paid within $N$. A 12-bit profile ID, CRC16, and terminated rate-1/2 convolutional code occupy 68 rotated-BPSK symbols $\{\pm(1+j)\}$. The remaining 956 symbols carry the LDPC-coded body. The paid profile identifies the actual endpoint and MCS.

The frozen VAR tokenizer produces ten residual token maps $q_s$, with side lengths $(1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10,13,16)$ and a 4096-entry codebook~\cite{var}. Let $L_s$ be the map size and $C_s$ its codebook lookup, resizing, and residual transform. The complete quantized latent is $f_q=\sum_s C_s(q_s)$. Image decoder $\Dc$ is adapted on 20\,000 ImageNet training images using continuous, fully quantized, and prefix-completion-interpolated latents, then fixed. NeST-Com uses null-class conditioning. Its frozen next-scale prior factorizes as
\begin{equation}
 p_\theta(q_s\mid q_{<s})=\prod_{i=1}^{L_s}p_\theta(q_{s,i}\mid q_{<s}),
 \label{eq:prior}
\end{equation}
providing the local cumulative distribution functions (CDFs) for arithmetic coding~\cite{arithmetic} at both ends of Fig.~\ref{fig:system}.

\subsection{Within-Scale Endpoints and Actual-Length Fitting}
An endpoint $e=(m,K)$ retains complete coarse maps and $K$ raster-ordered positions from the next map:
\begin{equation}
 \pi_e=(q_1,\ldots,q_m,q_{m+1,1:K}).
 \label{eq:prefix}
\end{equation}
WHOLE permits $K=0$. PARTIAL additionally uses
$K\in\{\lfloor L_{m+1}/4\rfloor,\lfloor L_{m+1}/2\rfloor,\lfloor3L_{m+1}/4\rfloor\}$ for $m=4,\ldots,9$; $(10,0)$ denotes the full representation.

For MCS $a=(M,r)$, the coded body has $n_a=956\log_2M$ bits and information length $k_a=\lfloor rn_a\rfloor$. With actual terminated arithmetic length $b(x,e)$,
\begin{equation}
 b(x,e)+29\leq k_a,\qquad \eta(x,e)=\frac{b(x,e)}{k_a-29}.
 \label{eq:budget}
\end{equation}
The 29 bits comprise the length field and CRC16; unused information positions are zero-padded before 5G NR LDPC coding, rate matching~\cite{nr}, and QAM. Thus $\eta$ measures entropy-stream capacity utilization.

The initial target search uses $m=7,8,9$, each with $K=0$ or the three quarter-scale counts, and 12 MCSs: $\log_2M\in\{2,4,6\}$ and $r\in\{1/2,2/3,3/4,5/6\}$. A 100-image pilot shortlists candidates; its top three and the complete-scale anchor are reevaluated on all 1000 calibration images. The subsequent full-$m=10$ revision retains these finalists and evaluates the distinct new full-scale policies after exact-stream reuse. Selection maximizes source/reconstruction DINOv2 cosine~\cite{dinov2}: average three actual channel outcomes per image, including rejected gray outputs, then average sources. Exact ties prefer legacy candidates, then lexical candidate ID. This is a staged search, not exhaustive full-population optimization.

At 4/10/19~dB, frozen WHOLE targets are $(7,0)/(9,0)/(10,0)$ and PARTIAL targets are $(7,25)/(8,126)/(10,0)$. Both select $(\log_2M,r)=(2,2/3)/(4,2/3)/(6,5/6)$, yielding source capacities 1245/2520/4751 bits. The repository records the complete candidate and fallback lists.

The fallback chain is deterministic, not an additional learned parameter: enumerate legal endpoints no longer than the target, order by decreasing token count, and stop at $(4,0)$. WHOLE uses complete scales; PARTIAL also uses the quarter endpoints above. Counts are distinct, so no endpoint-order tie occurs. With fixed MCS, TX sends the first independently terminated stream satisfying~\eqref{eq:budget}; its paid profile identifies the actual endpoint. No feasible endpoint triggers an execution error in this implementation, not a defined null transmission; all evaluated sources fit. A slice of a longer arithmetic stream is never used.

\subsection{Prefix-Constrained Reconstruction}
Invalid profile CRC/catalogue, body CRC, declared length, known padding, or arithmetic parsing produces a fixed gray image. Otherwise, the receiver decodes the prefix using CDFs rebuilt from the complete coarse maps actually received. It predicts the entire boundary map by VAR argmax conditioned on complete coarse maps, then applies known-token overwrite~\cite{var}:
\begin{equation}
 \tilde q_{m+1,i}=\begin{cases}\hat q_{m+1,i},&i\leq K,\\q^{\mathrm{pred}}_{m+1,i},&i>K.\end{cases}
 \label{eq:overwrite}
\end{equation}
Unreceived boundary positions retain that coarse-conditioned prediction; they are not recomputed conditioned on the received $K$ positions. The merged map contributes directly to the residual latent and updates the prior state before finer-scale prediction. Complete received maps remain unchanged; subsequent maps are generated from this updated context. Reconstruction is
\begin{equation}
 \hat x=\Dc\!\left(\sum_{s=1}^{10}C_s(\tilde q_s)\right).
 \label{eq:reconstruction}
\end{equation}
At the final scale, overwrite changes the residual contribution without any subsequent propagation.
