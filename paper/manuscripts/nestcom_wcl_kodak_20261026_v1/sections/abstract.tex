\begin{abstract}
Next-scale tokens support progressive image transmission, but whole-scale endpoints can leave digital-frame capacity unused. NeST-Com transmits within-scale token prefixes, fits their terminated entropy-coded streams to a fixed symbol budget, and reconstructs missing tokens using a shared frozen prior. With the same prior and decoder, within-scale endpoints raise source-bit utilization from 78\% to 95\% at 10~dB and improve PSNR by 0.32--0.70~dB over whole-scale endpoints. For 1024 complex symbols per image, NeST-Com achieves the lowest mean DreamSim and highest mean CLIP-I among five methods on ImageNet at all three SNRs; continuous JSCC offers higher low-SNR PSNR and lower processing cost.
\end{abstract}
