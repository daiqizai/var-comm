\begin{abstract}
NeST-Com targets perceptually faithful image reconstruction within fixed digital frames. It fits independently terminated within-scale prefixes of a next-scale residual representation and uses received boundary tokens for residual reconstruction and finer-scale conditioning. With shared models on 500 ImageNet images, LPIPS and DreamSim decrease across all tested SNRs at 512 and 1024 symbols, alongside 0.32--0.79~dB PSNR gains. At 1024 symbols and 10~dB, source-bit utilization rises from 77.70\% to 95.21\%, while mean LPIPS and DreamSim fall by 13.7\% and 18.3\%. Comparisons with existing systems reveal perceptual and pixel fidelity tradeoffs.
\end{abstract}
