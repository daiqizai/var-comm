\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com fits independently terminated within-scale prefixes into fixed digital frames and uses received boundary tokens for residual correction and finer-scale conditioning. Shared-model experiments at 512 and 1024 symbols show higher source-bit utilization and consistent improvements in source-referenced LPIPS and DreamSim. Complete-system comparisons demonstrate the value of this limited-budget reconstruction route, with perceptual and pixel fidelity tradeoffs that depend on SNR and the baseline. Future work will extend frame selection to uncertain channel conditions.
