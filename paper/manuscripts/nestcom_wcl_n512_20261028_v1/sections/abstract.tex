\begin{abstract}
Next-scale tokens describe images through coarse-to-fine residual maps, but complete-scale endpoints can underfill fixed digital frames. NeST-Com fits independently terminated within-scale prefixes and uses received boundary tokens for residual reconstruction and finer-scale prediction. At 1024 symbols, shared-model comparisons improve PSNR by 0.32--0.70~dB and raise source-bit utilization from 77.70\% to 95.21\% at 10~dB. Six-system comparisons reveal pixel--perceptual tradeoffs. With independently calibrated 512-symbol policies, NeST exceeds a fixed-frame Ada-TokenCom adaptation by 1.74--4.18~dB PSNR, while Ada retains higher CLIP image similarity at all tested SNRs and lower DreamSim at 19~dB.
\end{abstract}
