\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com fits coarse-to-fine residual tokens to fixed digital frames through within-scale endpoints, actual terminated lengths, and prefix-constrained reconstruction. Shared-model results connect improved utilization to quality gains, with received boundary tokens affecting both residual reconstruction and finer-scale context. Gains over whole-scale transmission persist at 512 symbols. External comparisons retain pixel--perceptual tradeoffs, including Ada's stronger CLIP-I at that budget. Policies for uncertain SNR remain future work.
