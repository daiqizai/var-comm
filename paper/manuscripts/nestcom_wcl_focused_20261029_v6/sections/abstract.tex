\begin{abstract}
NeST-Com uses next-scale residual tokens for digital image communication under a fixed per-image symbol budget. Within-scale endpoints fit independently terminated entropy-coded prefixes to available payloads; received boundary tokens update residual reconstruction and finer-scale prediction. Against whole-scale transmission with shared models at 512 and 1024 symbols, LPIPS and DreamSim decrease at all matched SNRs, with PSNR gains of 0.32--0.79~dB. At 1024 symbols and 10~dB, source-bit utilization rises from 77.70\% to 95.21\%, while LPIPS and DreamSim fall by 13.7\% and 18.3\%. Complete-system comparisons characterize the resulting quality tradeoffs.
\end{abstract}
