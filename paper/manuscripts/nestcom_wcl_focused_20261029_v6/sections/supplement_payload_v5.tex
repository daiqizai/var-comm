\clearpage\subsection*{S1.3. ARPC caption and visual payload accounting}
This descriptive audit uses the existing \texttt{external\_common100} sources contained in the common500 study. For each source and SNR, the recorded endpoint is joined to the original source-container breakdown. The 900 source/SNR/repetition identities agree with the common500 reception records, and the 300 source/SNR container lengths agree with the source encoder records. Repetitions of an identical source payload are counted once when computing the equal-source means.

The encoded caption content averages 258.64 bits, with a range of 96--424 bits. A one-bit coding-mode flag and nine-bit length field bring the mean caption contribution to 268.64 bits. The table reports the source container before the outer 29-bit length/CRC overhead, LDPC, and protected physical profile.
