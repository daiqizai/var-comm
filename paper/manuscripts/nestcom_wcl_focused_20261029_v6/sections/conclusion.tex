\section{Conclusion}
NeST-Com enables next-scale image communication within fixed digital frames through within-scale prefixes, actual terminated-length fitting, and prefix-constrained reconstruction. Shared-model controls at 512 and 1024 symbols show that additional boundary information improves source-bit utilization and perceptual reconstruction. Comparisons with digital adaptations of ARPC and Ada-TokenCom, conventional coding, and JSCC establish the system's perceptual and pixel-fidelity tradeoffs.
