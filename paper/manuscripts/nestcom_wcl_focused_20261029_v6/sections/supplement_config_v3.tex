SwinJSCC is trained for 200,000 updates and selected using the mean RGB MSE over 1,000 calibration images, two symbol budgets, and six SNRs. Its late-stage training loss remains near 0.0061, and mean calibration MSE decreases by 0.20\% over the final 20,000 updates. The selected checkpoint, training curves, and stopping records remain linked in S1; the accompanying \texttt{inputs/training/} directory provides the original numerical records and the aggregation audit. The decoder adaptation for NeST/WHOLE does not define a separately trained coarse-only branch.
