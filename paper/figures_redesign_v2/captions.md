# English figure captions

**Figure 2.** Reconstruction performance at N=1024 on the original common500 images. Markers show source-averaged means and error bars indicate pointwise 95% source-level bootstrap intervals; each source is first averaged over three channel realizations, retaining failures. The entropy-coded method uses the corrected m10-enabled policy at 19 dB. Latent-space JSCC and adapted SwinJSCC use their final 100k and 200k checkpoints, respectively. SNR positions are numerically spaced; straight segments connect measured points only. Prediction agreement compares the classifier predictions of the reconstruction and source, and is not label accuracy.

**Figure 3.** Paired effects of within-scale truncation on the original common500 images at N=1024, evaluated separately for raw and entropy-coded source representations. Markers show mean paired differences; error bars denote pointwise 95% source-paired bootstrap intervals after averaging three channel realizations per source. Positive values indicate higher PSNR (partial minus whole) or lower DreamSim (whole minus partial). The entropy-coded 19 dB comparison includes m10 in both candidate families. Horizontal positions represent three categorical test operating points, with small series offsets for clarity. Each family's original calibrated transmission settings are retained; these comparisons do not hold every MCS variable constant and do not isolate the causal contribution of entropy coding.

All means and interval bounds are read from completed reports. No scoring or resampling was performed for these figures. The selected DreamSim mathematical settings and source-input compatibility are documented in `review/dreamsim_identity.json`. No interpretation of m10 prevalence is inferred from these curves. Historical qualitative13dB raw images and H800 timing are unchanged and are outside this package.
