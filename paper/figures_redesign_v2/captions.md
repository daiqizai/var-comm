# English figure captions

**Figure 2.** Reconstruction performance at N=1024 on 500 images. Markers show source-averaged means and error bars indicate pointwise 95% source-level bootstrap intervals after averaging three channel realizations per source. Latent-space JSCC and adapted SwinJSCC use their final 100k and 200k checkpoints, respectively. Agreement is the fraction of images whose ConvNeXt top-1 prediction matches that of the source image.

**Figure 3.** Paired effects of within-scale truncation on 500 images at N=1024, evaluated separately for raw and entropy-coded representations. ΔPSNR = PSNR(partial) − PSNR(whole); ΔDreamSim = DreamSim(whole) − DreamSim(partial), so positive values indicate improvement. Markers show mean paired differences; error bars indicate pointwise 95% source-paired bootstrap intervals after averaging three channel realizations per source.
