# Experimental settings note

Both figures use only the original common500 sources, N=1024, and three channel realizations per source. At 19 dB, both entropy-coded candidate families include m10 and use the frozen T1-C corrected evaluation. The 4/10 dB entropy results and final P100k/Swin200k baselines retain their corresponding completed evaluations. Historical raw within-family comparisons use the verified compatible DreamSim implementation, weights, preprocessing and source inputs.

The family comparisons retain their original calibrated transmission settings. They compare partial versus whole source representations within each family; they do not isolate entropy coding while holding every MCS variable constant. All means and intervals are copied from completed reports. No scoring or resampling was performed for these figures. Population/version and per-row provenance remain in the input manifests. Historical qualitative images and H800 timing are unchanged.
