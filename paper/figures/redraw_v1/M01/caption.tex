System overview of NeST-Com. The frozen visual encoder produces a multiscale residual-quantized representation with scale widths 1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10,13,16. A preselected prefix (m,K) is coded as fixed 12-bit indices or using optional transmitter-side VAR-conditional entropy coding. The paid header communicates the decoding profile; it is not free side information. Known padding may carry energy. The raw protocol retains actual hard-decision body tokens after body CRC rejection (KEEP); entropy decoding uses its own frozen rejection rules. Receiver prefix overwrites change the accumulated latent and condition finer scales, without recomputing other positions in the same boundary scale. The transmitter knows the lookup SNR and selects its prefix before transmission; arbitrary mid-codeword air-interface interruption is not supported. Dc was adapted previously and is now frozen.
