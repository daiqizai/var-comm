Empirical distributions of source-capacity utilization over common500, counting each source once per family and SNR (3,000 records). Utilization is the actual terminated arithmetic bitstream length divided by k - 13 - 16; byte padding is not charged as arithmetic bits. Both families use the same frozen H800 source implementation. The original 4/10 dB fallback policies and the corrected T1-C m10 policies at 19 dB are retained. The curves describe source packing, not wireless image quality. Historical 4/10 dB whole-scale quality may only be associated with these curves after the separately documented event-version repair. No source, channel, reconstruction, metric, or bootstrap call is added by this plot.
