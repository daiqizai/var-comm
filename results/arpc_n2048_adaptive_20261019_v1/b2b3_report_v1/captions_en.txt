Calibration. Actual received-pixel DINO averaged over three noises within each source, then over 1,000 calibration sources. Nine registered candidates; filled circles denote the saved four selections. No calibration confidence intervals were calculated.

Adaptive quality. Seven metrics for the frozen finite policies at 4 and 10 dB on external_common100_v1. Each source first averages three noises. Error bars are the saved pointwise 95% source-bootstrap intervals. Failures retain their original denominators and fixed receiver outputs. Agreement is the fraction of reconstructions whose ConvNeXt top-1 prediction matches that of the source.

Direct paired differences. Saved NeST-Com minus ARPC paired estimates and 95% intervals. Agreement differences are in percentage points (pp); CSV values retain their original fractions. Positive values favor NeST-Com for PSNR, prediction agreement, DINO and MS-SSIM; negative values favor NeST-Com for LPIPS, DreamSim and DISTS. Intervals touching zero do not establish equivalence. The separate fixed complete-representation 19 dB table is not a low-SNR-adapted result.
