# R0 gap and bounded screening proposal

Status: proposal only; additional scientific calls are NOT AUTHORIZED or RUN.
The authorized R1 and conditional R2 budgets are unchanged.

## Verified gap

The catalogue has all 433 original single-group actions, 201 legal unequal
two-group actions and 55 legal equal-rate two-group actions. The archived
BLER tables fully cover only 186/433, 2/201 and 16/55 respectively across the
registered true SNRs. The union lacks 1,852 exact layout/SNR points at
4, 7, 10 and 13 dB; unmeasured probabilities have not been assigned zero or one.

The last targeted archive audit recovered 17 raster-prefix states on all 200
fixed calibration sources, using exact equality of every actually received
token with the frozen source tokens. Repeats of a source/state had identical
reconstruction hashes and DINO scores; the first archived record was retained.
These historical GPU results are screening references, not new H800 results.
The remaining clean-Q gap is 27 states times 200 sources = 5,400 DINO-scored
clean VAR reconstructions. Five states (1,000 calls) belong to the grouped
endpoint set; restricting work to them would leave single-group permissions
without comparable screening evidence.

## Proposed finite addition, if approved

Only SNR 4/7/10 enters the prespecified screening objective. Defer 13 dB table
work: it is a pressure condition in the already-authorized actual R1 grid.
The three selection SNRs have 1,389 missing exact layout/SNR points. Obtain
64 predetermined independent packet trials per missing point, capped at
88,896 additional body-packet trials. Use the original backend/QAM/CRC,
record rejection and undetected error separately, and report binomial
uncertainty. Do not adapt trial count after seeing results or retry completed
trials. Existing compatible table entries remain unchanged.

Evaluate only the remaining 5,400 clean source/state pairs under the frozen
model and DINO identities. Authenticate every reused pair first; use no
development or holdout image or score for this step. No training, new method,
new SNR, bootstrap or inference beyond this declared screening addition.

Before any calls, freeze a separate screening registration, seed namespace,
exact missing keys, identities, call ledgers, resource guard and nomination
rule. The estimated DROP mixture is a screening approximation only. It does
not assign gray quality to the actual KEEP arm, prove global optimality, or
decide GO. The limited S candidates still receive actual KEEP/DROP evaluation
in R1, and mandatory nominal/backoff KEEP outcomes remain eligible strong-
single winners. Finite nomination uncertainty and unequal historical/new
BLER sample sizes must be reported.

After this addition, original R1 remains at most 36,000 physical frames and
46,800 logical VAR outputs. Original R2 remains conditional on the fixed gate.
No expansion is hidden inside those budgets.

## Alternative

A narrower, data-independent geometrical shortlist could be frozen instead.
That changes the screening scope and supports only that restricted diagnostic;
it cannot be described as equal empirical screening over the full catalogue.
No such shortlist has been selected or run.

## Why confirmation is needed

The supplied plan permits supplementing tables only for a few candidates
affecting nomination, and its execution instructions say to report out-of-scope
gaps before continuing. The verified missing set is substantially larger than
that description. This document makes the proposed extra cost explicit; it
does not treat an entry script or CPU tests as experimental completion.
