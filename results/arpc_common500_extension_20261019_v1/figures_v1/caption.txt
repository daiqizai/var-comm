Fig. 2. Five-method performance on the original common500 population at N = 1024 complex channel symbols. Points average three noise realizations within each source and then average 500 sources; error bars show pointwise 95% source-bootstrap intervals using the registered 10,000 × 500 index bank. Agreement is the fraction of images whose ConvNeXt top-1 prediction matches that of the source image. NeST-Com uses the T1-C m10-corrected entropy-coded partial-scale result at 19 dB; the continuous baselines use the fixed P100k and Swin200k checkpoints. ARPC uses its frozen N1024 policies and actual per-image payload fallback. All receive failures remain in the denominator.
