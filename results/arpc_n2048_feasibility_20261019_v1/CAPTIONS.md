**ARPC source payload–quality on fixed cal100.** Each point is the arithmetic
mean of the same 100 sources at one complete native scale endpoint, e1–e7.
Only e7 includes the full seven-scale representation. Payload includes the
transmitted caption, source syntax, native termination and source alignment;
outer channel headers and CRCs are accounted for separately in the layout table.
Horizontal bars show observed source-payload minima and maxima, not confidence
intervals. Lines join measured endpoint means. PSNR, LPIPS-Alex and DINOv2-L
cosine scores are the existing clean-reconstruction scores. These curves do
not measure wireless performance. No new scoring or resampling was performed.
