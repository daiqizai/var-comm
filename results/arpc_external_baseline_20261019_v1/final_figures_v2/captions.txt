100-source matched comparison. The fixed external_common100_v1 subset is drawn from the original common500 population. All methods use the same100 source IDs; each source averages three noise realizations. Error bars are pointwise95% source-bootstrap percentile intervals (10000 resamples, seed2026100701). NeST-Com19dB uses the revised T1-C m10 policy; Latent-space JSCC and SwinJSCC use their final100k and200k checkpoints. Agreement is the fraction of images whose ConvNeXt top-1 prediction matches that of the source image. ARPC uses the official adapted weights with the paid caption/container and full N1024 frame, including failure cases.

Paired source differences. Each difference is NeST-Com (entropy-coded) minus ARPC before the same source resampling. Intervals are directly paired, pointwise and unadjusted. Negative differences favor NeST-Com for LPIPS, DreamSim and DISTS; positive differences favor it for PSNR, DINO-L, agreement and MS-SSIM. Agreement differences are percentage points. Historical baseline noise seeds differ, so pairing is by source. This100-source comparison is not the full500-source result or a fresh blind test.
