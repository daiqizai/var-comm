# ARPC A1: independent native qualification

Status: **READY_FOR_CALIBRATION**. This is an eight-source, no-channel qualification,
not a wireless comparison or a claim that ARPC outperforms another system.

All 24 independent prefix cases (endpoints 1, 4, 7 for each fixed source) passed exact
caption bytes, integer CDFs, prefix tokens and uint8 image checks; float errors are
within the registered 1e-6 bound. TX, RX, CPU audit and owner waits actually returned
zero and the recorded processes are gone. The root opened the eight-row contact
sheet and found no obvious incorrect weight loading or native-resolution failure.
Full-scale images preserve the source scene; shallow generations may alter content.
No output or source was selected away because of quality or capacity.

The originally selected NVML UUID could not initialize CUDA. That first attempt
failed before its call ledger or model loads. The separate resource registration
uses index 4 and checks the exact PCI bus and CUDA UUID before each frozen entry.
Both attempts and their actual waits remain available. No completed scientific call
was repeated, and the original absolute qualification deadline was retained.

Caption content costs 160–352 bits (mean 261) on these eight sources. It dominates
the shallowest endpoint and is fully paid. Endpoint 4 averages 896.25 author-counted

metadata and does not claim that the container is globally minimal.

## Actual work and resources

8 captions, 8 tokenizations, 8 sender probability traversals, 24 reference images
and 24 independent RX prefix decodes/images completed. There were zero wireless
frames, score calls, bootstrap calls or training updates. The VAE has all 268
required parameter keys; the native strict prior loader succeeded.

TX wall time was 80.686 s, RX 53.899 s, and audit 1.122 s, including their respective
initialization and I/O. Peak allocated/reserved memory was 19.589/23.537 GiB. Per-call
resource records are included separately. These are planning measurements on leo,
not new H800 latency figures or cross-device speed ratios.

A2a can reuse all eight source passes and the 24 checked endpoint states, so at most
92 new source passes and 676 new clean endpoint states are needed to reach the
fixed cal100 × 7 screen. Actual identity checks remain required. A2b retains the
original cal1000, at most two candidates per SNR and 18,000 logical frames; the
independent communication qualification remains at most 24 frames. A3 remains 900
logical frames on the registered external_common100 subset. Nothing in this gate
substitutes for matched reliability coverage or permits new model training.
