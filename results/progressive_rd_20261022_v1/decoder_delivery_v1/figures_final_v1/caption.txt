Calibration100 results after 5,000 matched decoder updates per arm. The eight points retain the registered complete/within-scale endpoints and population mean terminated arithmetic rates. Overview error bars are pointwise 95% source-bootstrap intervals. Paired panels use direct within-source differences and the same single registered bootstrap bank; Δ is the first named arm minus the second. No score, bootstrap bank, endpoint, model or sampling setting is recomputed by this plotter. These calibration results do not establish held-out or wireless performance.
