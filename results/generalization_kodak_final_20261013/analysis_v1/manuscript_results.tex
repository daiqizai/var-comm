We evaluated the frozen systems on all 24 previously fixed Kodak center crops ($256\times256$), using $N=1024$, SNRs of 4, 10, and 19 dB, and three prescribed noise realizations per source. The final latent-space JSCC and SwinJSCC checkpoints were transferred without Kodak training or calibration. NeST-Com (raw) is compared with both final baselines and adaptive-resolution BPG + LDPC; relative to latent-space JSCC (100k), its source-paired differences at 19 dB are +0.630 dB, -0.035, +0.197, +19.444 percentage points for PSNR, LPIPS, DINOv2-L cosine similarity, and ConvNeXt prediction agreement, respectively; relative to adapted SwinJSCC (200k), its source-paired differences at 19 dB are -1.729 dB, -0.339, +0.285, +30.556 percentage points for PSNR, LPIPS, DINOv2-L cosine similarity, and ConvNeXt prediction agreement, respectively. Positive differences favor NeST-Com except LPIPS, where negative is better. The complete three-SNR results and pointwise source-bootstrap intervals are reported in the accompanying table; this small fixed-crop benchmark supports a limited cross-collection comparison.
