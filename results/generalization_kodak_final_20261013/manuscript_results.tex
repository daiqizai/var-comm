On all 24 fixed Kodak center crops ($256\times256$), the frozen ImageNet-calibrated systems were tested at $N=1024$ and 4/10/19 dB, with three prescribed noise realizations per source and no Kodak training or selection. At 19 dB, NeST-Com (raw) reached 22.416 dB PSNR, 0.133 LPIPS, 0.851 DINOv2-L cosine similarity, and 62.5\% source-prediction agreement. Against adapted SwinJSCC-200k, the source-paired PSNR difference was -1.729 dB (95\% CI [-1.997, -1.406]), while the DINOv2-L difference was +0.285 (95\% CI [+0.226, +0.348]). The tradeoff was not uniform: NeST-Com had lower PSNR than SwinJSCC and adaptive BPG at all three SNRs; at 4 dB it also had lower PSNR and higher LPIPS than latent-space JSCC-100k. The 19-dB agreement difference against latent-space JSCC had a confidence interval including zero. All failures were retained, including four historical raw CRC rejections. These 24 fixed center crops support a limited cross-collection evaluation, not a native-resolution Kodak claim.
