timing experiment or bootstrap is called.

Only the twenty missing receiver outputs are reconstructed, on the original
PRO6000 GPU1, with original per-method Python/software, checkpoint, FP32/TF32

status. A mismatch or unknown failure stops the finite owner without retry.

process. At most twenty matching PSNR/LPIPS pairs may be computed, using the
original released definitions and weights; no three-noise mean is a display
score. PNGs are uniformly rounded 8-bit display copies of the saved native
float32 reconstructions, without crop, sharpening or generative changes.

Only sources, registrations, lightweight reports and final PNG materials enter
Git. Previous timing, scientific results, failures and the open Table I remain
unchanged. The output is a complete candidate sheet, not a claim of population
statistics or cross-method identical noise realizations.
